ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

"WE ARE THE FUTURE OF DEFENCE AND AEROSPACE EXCELLENCE"

Introducing ourselves, then we are Imperial Armoury Private Limited, a beacon of innovation and excellence in India's defense and aerospace sector. Defense forces, law enforcement agencies, and other security agencies now use our expertise in the design, development, and manufacturing of cutting-edge ammunition and firearms.

We feel proud, and of course, take pride in empowering defense forces with ammunition that are manufactured and developed with precision. Delivering our reliable products to the security forces of India, we are strategically based in Jodhpur, Rajasthan. We are focusing on an approach to safeguarding the future through world-class defense solutions.
