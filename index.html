About Us

Imperial Armoury Private Limited was founded in the historic
city of Jodhpur, Rajasthan, driven by a singular vision to
revolutionize indigenous defense manufacturing in India. Within
just a few years of inception, we successfully expanded our
footprint across the national defense and sporting arms
sectors. Embodying the spirit of 'Aatmanirbhar Bharat'
(Self-Reliant India), our company has grown from a specialized
workshop into a formidable manufacturing enterprise. Today,
we boast extensive research and development facilities
alongside a state-of-the-art logistics and distribution center,
enabling us to meet the rigorous demands of both civilian and
military clients with uncompromising precision.

The precision machining of our firearms takes place almost
entirely at our advanced headquarters in Jodhpur. Here, critical
components such as upper and lower receivers, tactical
handguards, rail systems, and specialized stocks are
engineered and manufactured utilizing our proprietary
machinery and advanced CNC technology. Intricate mechanical
finishing, including vibratory grinding, hard-coat anodizing, and
robot-assisted glass bead blasting, is seamlessly integrated
into our workflow. Our production floor relies entirely on top-tier
global machining centers to ensure microscopic tolerances.
